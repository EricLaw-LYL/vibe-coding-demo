06 / KEEP THESE CLOSE

Stay curious.
Stay in the driver’s seat.

01

Make it small.

Start with one job the app should do. Add the next feature after the first one works.

02

Ask one thing.

Change one thing at a time. It’s easier to see what helped and what broke.

03

Save a good version.

Keep a working copy before big changes. Experimenting feels better with a way back.

04

Use made-up data.

Learn with sample names and content. Keep passwords, private files, and sensitive information out of prompts.
